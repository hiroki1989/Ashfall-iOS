# Multicolor weapon art / 多色武器原画

Built-in imagegen, precise-object-edit. Original Weapons_0..5.png preserved; only new ColorArt assets are written. No CLI and no programmatic PNG editing. Analysis uses Pillow/NumPy/SciPy read-only; JSON is regenerated independently. Runtime/shader integration belongs to PMO.

## Contract / 契約
Six 1254×1254 RGBA atlases. Each 4×4 atlas contains two weapon models, eight views each; rows1–2 even model, rows3–4 odd model. Read order and gun shapes remain visually aligned with originals. Each gun separates natural dark iron, cold steel, warm brown wood/leather, ivory cloth/resin and a restrained painted or glass accent. Original design is retained; generated color-only editing is not pixel-exact alpha preservation.

Add restrained painted accent ONLY to existing small panel surfaces, and subtly colored glass or energy small details. DO NOT broadly tint the entire gun, do not make all cyan/blue/sepia, no rainbow/neon flooding. Do not add/delete/change shapes, scope, accessories or any details. No UI, text, logos, watermark, gridlines. Background fully transparent.
