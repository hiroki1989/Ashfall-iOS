# Fieldkit carbine art update / 2026-10-15

The wasteland key art shows the signature field carbine carried by the protagonist: a worn olive-drab handguard and stock, a blued-steel receiver, a compact cyan optic, and paired amber pressure vials above the receiver. The matching directional weapon artwork now loads for firearm model 12 in gameplay, inventory-derived runtime art, and the firing pose. The existing pump shotgun views remain unchanged.

Source: `Assets/Resources/Art/LayeredPlayer/InkedArt/Weapons_6.png`.
Runtime texture: `Assets/Resources/Art/LayeredPlayer/InkedArt/Fieldkit_Weapons_6.png` (8 carbine views followed by 8 pump-shotgun views, transparent RGBA, same atlas cells and weapon silhouettes). `LayeredPlayer2D` selects the new resource when present, with the original directional asset as fallback. Existing source art is retained.

## 2026-10-15: design reference correction
The earlier `Fieldkit_Weapons_6` pass interpreted the hero weapon as a modern olive polymer carbine; that interpretation was incorrect and is superseded. The user's menu reference is the weathered wooden-stock long rifle visible in `WastelandKeyArt.png`. Gameplay model 12 now loads the new sibling `InkedArt/Menu_Weapons_6.png`, whose first eight cells reproduce that wooden/steel/amber-vial rifle from all directions; its shotgun half remains copied from `Weapons_6.png`. `Fieldkit_Weapons_6.png` remains on disk as an unused historical asset and is no longer preferred by the loader.
