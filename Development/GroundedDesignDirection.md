# Grounded equipment direction — 2026-10-19

WastelandKeyArt.png is the governing world reference: modern field gear repaired after societal collapse. Black/blued steel, oiled wood, olive canvas, leather webbing, buckles, rust at exposed edges. Retrofuture devices are functional serviceable additions: vacuum tubes, ceramic insulators, glass reservoirs, pressure gauges and hydraulic dampers. No clean space suits, monolithic powered exoskeletons or pervasive neon.

| Maker | Construction | Performance expressed by construction |
|---|---|---|
| Kestrel | Modern precision weapons; walnut, dark steel, measuring dials | Accuracy, fast servicing; lower impact |

Named legendary guns and armor each use a dedicated complete illustration and a dedicated whole-item construction. Ordinary recipe components must not be their source. Existing stable legendary IDs and effects are retained, saved rolled statistics are not rerolled. New gun bases follow rifle / handgun / shotgun / LMG / SMG, with one complete tuned mechanism per maker. Their activation triggers remain third shot / hit / kill / reload / dash; armor triggers remain damage / kill / reload / dash / periodic combat.

Cinder's first legendary rifle recreates the menu weapon: skeletal wooden stock, long steel action/barrel, two upright amber glass chambers, brass plumbing, perforated muzzle brake. Its five armor pieces recreate the menu's round goggles and twin-filter respirator, worn olive jacket with asymmetrical salvaged shoulder, webbing/pouches, bracers, knee plates and lace-up combat boots. This is an obtainable named equipment set; no existing player's inventory is forcibly replaced.

The generated illustration sheets contain whole items (five guns on top; five armor slots below). This is storage packing, not modular illustration assembly. Runtime portraits crop one complete item. Dedicated model functions preserve the same identity through equipped and dropped representations.

Implementation and artwork in progress. Do not report full visual fidelity from successful compilation or catalog counts.
